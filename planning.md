# Planning

## Versioning
0.1.x is the foundation and early game, shipped. 0.2 is planets, orbit, orbital
science, and late game content. Do not advertise 0.2 as shipped.

## Milestones
1. M0 Verify the foundation in Factorio. Blocking. Only JJ can launch the game.
2. M1 Real ascent to orbit as a tiled space surface.
3. M2 Orbital science tier, original, made in orbit.
4. M3 Travel to planets by an original mechanic. Colonies, not piloted ships.
5. M4 Planet paradigms. Each planet forces a different way to build. The core
   differentiator.
6. M5 Content and polish. Real art, menu and main menu polish, balance, locale.

## Active lanes
Main session takes planet terrain per class and new tiles. The manager team
takes the blueprint break restructure and the Core Tap, design first, until M0
passes.

## Science tiers (drafted in game, 1 to 8)
The full chain is in mod/prototypes/science-tree.lua and shows in the tech tree.
Rocket, Orbital, six planet sciences (vacuum, cryo, magma, tide, gravity,
verdant), Resonance, Core, Exotic, three force sciences (gravitic, thermal,
flux), and Stellar. Each is a pack plus a gateway tech. Costs and recipes are
placeholder draft balance. Tiers 9 and 10 (Ascendant, Convergence) are roadmap
only.

# JJtorio

A work in progress overhaul for Factorio 2.0. An endless procedural universe
where every planet forces a different way to build. Inspired by the shape of
Space Exploration, never its systems. Standalone, not compatible with Space Age
or Space Exploration.

## Playable today (0.1.x)
- Light, faster early game. More reach, cheaper faster research, quicker tier 1
  machines, and a bigger starting inventory.
- The rocket silo is earned, gated behind a mature base, the full science line,
  electric furnaces, bots, express belts, and nuclear.

## Planned for 0.2 and beyond (NOT in the game yet)
- Endless procedural planets, each with its own class, terrain, resources, day
  length, and gravity.
- Reach orbit, a real space surface, and build an original orbital science tier.
- Planets that each force a different way to build, a constraint like heat,
  gravity, or storms that breaks blueprint copy paste. The core idea.
- An early game restructured so vanilla blueprints do not just paste in.
- A new core mining scale extraction mechanic tied to each planet's traits, plus
  custom biome and space tiles.

## Compatibility
Standalone. Not compatible with Space Age or Space Exploration. Targets Factorio
2.0.

## Status
Early work in progress by a young solo modder, with a little help. 0.1.x is the
foundation and early game. The space content above is the 0.2 goal, not yet
playable.

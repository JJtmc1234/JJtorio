# Brainstorm

How we arrived at JJtorio.

## The idea
JJ wanted a fun, fairly complex Factorio overhaul. We chose a space overhaul
shaped like Space Exploration rather than Space Age, so an endless procedural
universe instead of a few handmade planets.

## Key choices
1. Early game stays close to vanilla but lighter and faster. Depth lives later,
   in space.
2. Keep the vanilla rocket silo as the route to space, gated behind a mature
   base.
3. Space is a navigable tiled surface. After the silo you reach orbit and make a
   new orbital science.
4. Every planet forces a different way to build, so blueprints do not copy paste
   between worlds.

## Originality rule
Space Exploration inspires the shape, never the systems. We drop its four
sciences, piloted spaceships, and data and energy beaming. We invent our own
mechanics, like a Core Tap keyed to each planet's rolled facts. You plan self
running colonies, you do not pilot a ship.

## Compatibility
Standalone. Not compatible with Space Age or Space Exploration. Targets Factorio
2.0.
